# Reconstruction material source and prompts

Tool: built-in image_gen. The approved concept (`art/reference/abandoned-quarry-approved.png`) was supplied as a style/material reference for both requests, not an edit target. Both outputs are opaque seamless material tiles. Source PNGs are in `art/sprites/terrain/quarry/`; runtime WebPs are in `assets/ground/`. Resize each source to 512 x 512 using Lanczos and encode WebP at quality 94, method 6. The new filenames preserve the earlier material assets.

## quarry-bedrock-v2

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless game terrain material, not a scene. Reference image is STYLE AND MATERIAL reference only. Generate a square seamless repeating TOP SURFACE quarry bedrock texture matching the broad grey flag-like natural rock plates on the reference's work terrace. Flat orthographic overhead view. Large irregular fractured slate-grey and warm-grey limestone plates, each several times wider than the small gravel in typical game textures, restrained dark cracks, warm dust in some joints, subtle subdued moss in very few cracks. Six to ten broad plates across the full image, readable large shapes with hand-painted pixel-art shading and restrained dark outlines. Quiet matte surfaces, no small pebble noise or busy repeated pavers. Grey rock dominates; ochre dust only a secondary material. Uniform diffuse top-left light, no cast shadows. No cliffs or vertical surfaces, grass borders, props, text, perspective, scene, or vignette. Fill the entire square edge to edge; seamless tile boundaries. Match the reference's material quality, not its layout.

## quarry-cliff-v2

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless vertical quarry cliff face material for a 2D RPG terrain renderer. Reference image establishes the exact desired rock material. Produce a square edge-to-edge FRONT-ON rock face texture, not a scene. Broad irregular slate-grey and warm-grey limestone columns split by deep vertical and diagonal fractures, large chiseled quarry faces with chunky hand-painted planes. Muted grey dominates, with a broken narrow ochre mineral seam crossing horizontally around the middle. Small restrained moss in a few crevices. Match the reference's tall quarry walls with broad blocky natural rock forms. Use hand-painted pixel-art shading, upper-left diffuse light and clean readable forms, no photographic noise. Seamless left and right boundaries. No floor or top surface, grass rim, trees, vegetation hanging beyond image, buildings, props, sky, text or framing. Avoid brown brick walls, regular masonry courses, tiny repeating stones or horizontal planks.
