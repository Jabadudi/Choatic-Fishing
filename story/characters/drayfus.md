# Drayfus - Character Sheet

**Role:** Leader of the Willowback village guard and Eli's and Mara's trainer. Drayfus is also the scarred swordsman who leaves Emilia's house in Chapter 4 (decided, see `story/README.md`).

**First appearance:** Chapter 4 (unnamed swordsman at Emilia's hearth). Named by Emilia at the end of Chapter 4. First lines of dialogue in Chapter 6.

## Appearance (as stated)
- Big. "Broad through the chest and shoulders in a way that didn't come from a gym" (Ch6). Taller and heavier than Eli.
- Short dark hair going gray at the sides (Ch6).
- A pale scar across the jaw, another along the forearm, and "a dozen smaller ones that looked like the kind of thing he didn't bother counting anymore" (Ch6).
- A very big sword strapped across his back, "taller than Mara" (Ch6). Plain.
- Age: about the age Eli's parents would have been, mid-fifties (decided). Oscar is 20 to 30 years older than him.
- Lands lightly for a man his size (Ch7).

## Personality and voice
Blunt, economical, unimpressed, and secretly kind. Measures people. Rarely explains.
- "Sunrise was some time ago." (Ch6)
- "So did the Rimejaw we buried yesterday." (Ch6)
- "That is not a weapon... It's a stick with string on it." (Ch6)
- "Squat. Like you're sitting in a chair." (Ch7)
- "I was a boy. I was an idiot." (Ch7)
- Laughs once, at the rod, and then goes quiet when the line comes off his blade (Ch6).
- Explains the hollow in plain terms. He is no caster, but he has felt it himself after a long fight (Ch8).

## Relationships
- **Oscar:** Taught Drayfus to hold a blade, "so the chief tells me I owe him" (Ch6). The summer Oscar trained him, Drayfus watched him sit up at night talking to nothing (Ch7). Says "Oscar" quietly after the disarm (Ch6).
- **Emilia:** Takes her orders. She uses his name when he is rude to Eli (Ch6).
- **Eli:** Starts by measuring him as short-lived, then slowly takes him seriously. Trains him with buckets, laps and squats before letting him use the rod.
- **Mara:** Has known her all her life. He has seen her grow up running the perimeter since age nine (Ch7).
- **The village guard:** Leads it. Guards on the perimeter greet Eli as "Oscar's boy" (Ch7).

## Hooks
- Asked whether Oscar ever talked about Eli's grandmother, he freezes, then orders Eli to run (Ch7).
- Mara says he is afraid of what happens when he does tell (Ch7).
- He fought his first shadow walker younger than Mara is now (Ch6).

## Open questions / not yet established
- His eyes, skin tone and exact hair length.
- Whether he has a beard. The first reference sheet shows one, but the text never says so.
- His family, rank beyond "leads the village guard", and his weapon name.
- What he knows about Eli's grandmother.
