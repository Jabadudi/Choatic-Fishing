# The Talking Ocelot - Character Sheet

**Role:** Mysterious talking animal who appears right after Eli swallows the Region Core.

**First appearance:** Final lines of Chapter 5

## Appearance (as stated)
- "An ocelot. Spotted. Sleek." (Ch5)
- "Sitting there like it owned the place," in the corner of Eli's guest-lodge room (Ch5).

## Personality and voice
- It speaks: "It opened its mouth. And it *talked.*" What it said is not given. Its demeanor is self-assured, sitting "like it owned the place."

## Relationships
- **Eli:** Appears to him right after he ingests the core, while glowing writing floats before his eyes. He passes out.

## Hooks
- It appears at the exact moment of ingestion. A connection to the Region Core, the floating writing, or the voice is possible but not stated.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Its name, gender, voice, first words.
- Whether it is real, a spirit, a manifestation of the core, or connected to the woman's voice.
- Whether others can see it. Its size and coloring beyond "spotted."
- Whether it is an ordinary ocelot or a strange local hybrid like the Rimejaw or Flitlynx (the text says only "ocelot").

## Visual reference notes
Sleek, spotted ocelot sitting confidently in a corner, as if it owns the room. Eye color, any glow, and size are undefined.
