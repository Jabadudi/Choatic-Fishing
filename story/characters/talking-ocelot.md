# The Talking Ocelot - Character Sheet

**Role:** Mysterious spirit creature that speaks only to Eli. It appears right after Eli swallows the Region Core and acts as a dry-witted guide and teacher.

**First appearance:** Final lines of Chapter 5. First words in Chapter 6.

## Appearance (as stated)
- "An ocelot. Spotted. Sleek. Sitting there like it owned the place" (Ch5).
- Golden-brown, with dark rosettes along its back and two black stripes down each cheek, "like somebody had drawn on its eyeliner with a very steady hand" (Ch6).
- Blue eyes: "Not ocelot blue. Not any kind of animal blue. *Core* blue" (Ch6).
- Sits with its tail wrapped around its front paws.
- When it leaves it thins "like a reflection in a pond when someone drags a finger through the water," into blue motes, and a warmth settles in Eli's stomach where the core went (Ch6).

## Personality and voice
- Low, smooth, faintly bored, like "a butler who had been forced to work for a family he didn't respect." Accent unplaced. Dry and a little smug (Ch6).
- Teaches by blunt analogy: the heavy-box lesson about strength and power (Ch6).
- Calls Eli "boy" and "fool" until Ch8, when it says "Eli" for the first time, after the dark lure is found.
- Refuses to give its name: "Names have weight, and you couldn't carry a waterskin up a hill. Earn it." (Ch6)
- "You are sitting on the floor of another world, with a Region Core in your stomach... And the part you've decided to object to is the talking cat." (Ch6)
- When the dark lure appears: "Do not touch that." and "That should not be here." (Ch8). Afterward it says it is "thinking," and that Mara "is right about the husk, which is the part I do not like at all." (Ch9)

## Rules for how it works
- **Only Eli sees and hears it.** Mara looks straight at it and sees nothing (Ch6).
- It speaks in Eli's head, close and low, even in front of other people (Ch8).
- It dissolves in sunlight and seems to rest in Eli's stomach near the core (Ch6, Ch7, Ch9).

## Relationships
- **Eli:** Appears to him at the moment of ingestion. Critical, protective, a patient teacher.
- **Oscar and Drayfus:** It seems to know them. It tells Eli to "watch Drayfus's face" when he asks about his grandmother (Ch6, Ch7).
- **The Region Core:** Tied to it in some way. It goes silent when the real core goes silent (Ch8).
- **Mara and Emilia:** Mara cannot see it. Emilia guesses that something is talking to Eli (Ch9).

## Hooks
- It knows more than it says, and its knowledge reaches back before Eli: Oscar, Drayfus, husks.
- Its name is something Eli must earn.
- Its reaction to the husk suggests it knows what emptied the core.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Its name, age and where it comes from.
- Whether it is a spirit, a manifestation of the core, or a separate being.
- What it knows about husks, Croshelda and Goldanir.
- Why only Eli can see it.

## Visual reference notes
Book text says only: spotted, sleek, golden-brown with dark rosettes, black cheek stripes, bright blue eyes.
**Design addition (Netanel):** make it more mystical, a mix of ocelot and axolotl. The reference prompts add three feathery gill fronds behind each ear (coral-pink fading to glowing blue), translucent pale-pink belly and paws, a soft finned tail edge, and a faint glowing blue spot inside each rosette. This is not in the book text yet. If we keep it, a later chapter should mention the gills and fins.
