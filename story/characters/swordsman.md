# The Swordsman at Emilia's House - Character Sheet

**Role:** Unnamed warrior seen talking privately with Emilia.

**First appearance:** Chapter 4

## Appearance (as stated)
- A man standing beside Emilia at the hearth, talking in a low voice (Ch4).
- "Not a farmer. He had the build and the scars and the very big sword of someone who fought for a living" (Ch4).

## Personality and voice
- No dialogue. He gave Eli "a long, measuring glance on his way out, the kind that suggested he was working out how long I'd last in a fight, and that the number was short" (Ch4).

## Relationships
- **Emilia:** Confers with her in private. He leaves at "a quiet word" from her.
- **Eli:** Sizes him up, apparently unimpressed.

## Hooks
- What he and Emilia were discussing, possibly the previous night's attack.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Name. **Possibly Drayfus**, the trainer Emilia names minutes later. The text does not say so; treat it as unconfirmed.
- Face, hair, eyes, age, where his scars are, armor/clothing, what kind of sword it is beyond "very big."
- Rank or role (guard captain? village warrior?).
- Note: he is distinct from the two spear guards at the door. One of them has a scar through his eyebrow.

## Visual reference notes
Big, battle-scarred professional fighter with a very large sword and an appraising stare. Everything else is undefined. If he later turns out to be Drayfus, merge the designs.
