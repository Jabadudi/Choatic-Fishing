# Mara - Character Sheet

**Role:** Village girl and fighter who rescues Eli. Granddaughter of Chief Emilia. A human girl.

**First appearance:** End of Chapter 1 (named in Chapter 4)

## Appearance (as stated)
- "Around my age, maybe" (Ch1). "about my age, maybe a little younger" (Ch2).
- Long dark hair (Ch1). It is damp, pulled back and tied with what looks like a strip of leather (Ch2).
- After the night's fight: mud on her boots and sleeves, and a thin scratch across one cheekbone she doesn't seem to notice (Ch2).
- Eli finds her "Pretty. Really pretty, actually" (Ch1) and "kind of cute" when annoyed (Ch2).
- Eye color, height and build are not given.

## Personality and voice
Competent and matter-of-fact about monsters. She speaks of shadow walkers "the way you'd say *good thing you brought an umbrella*." Sarcastic and easily exasperated with Eli, but obviously worried about him. She moves through the forest "like she had a map of every root in her head." She corrects people (Rimejaw, not "grim-jaw"). When explaining shadow walkers her voice goes flat and quiet, like a lesson learned in childhood. She has a genuine, unsarcastic smile for her grandmother, and lights up "like someone had just handed her a birthday present" when training is announced.
- "You're finally awake." (Ch1)
- "This is no island." (Ch2)
- "Bed. Window. Chamber pot." / "*Sunrise,* Eli." (Ch5)

## Relationships
- **Emilia:** Her grandmother and the village chief.
- **Eli:** Rescued him and roasted a fish for him on Grandpa's rod. They forgot to introduce themselves until Ch4. She will apparently train alongside him.
- **The village:** Fought the shadow walker attack with the Moonfang and Rimejaws.

## What she knows / hooks
- Chased a shadow walker that broke away from the fight toward the shore. Says "Good thing I got to you in time" (Ch2).
- Says the cabin is "my grandma's old home" (Ch2).
- Knows the lore: Moonfang, Rimejaw, Flitlynx, Croshelda's dark army, Goldanir's disappearance 400 years ago.
- Says "If you really are his grandson, then I don't know how to help you get back." She recognizes the significance of Oscar's grandson before Ch4.
- Looked at the Region Core with awe and hope (Ch4).

## Open questions / not yet established
- Eye color, skin tone, height, build, clothing beyond boots and sleeves.
- Exact age; her parents; her weapon or fighting style.
- What she knew about Oscar before meeting Eli.
- Small continuity note: her first line is "You're finally awake." in Ch1 but "Are you finally awake?" in Ch2.

## Visual reference notes
Young woman about Eli's age with long dark hair tied back with a leather strip. Muddy boots and sleeves, a thin scratch on one cheekbone. Practical fighter/outdoors look; may hold Grandpa's fishing rod over a campfire. Eyes, coloring and outfit are undefined.
