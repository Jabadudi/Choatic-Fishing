# Greyhorn, the Hollow Elk - Character Sheet

**Role:** World boss of Elk Run. The lead bull of the Elk Spirit's herd, taken by the shadow and multiplied by it, as the Creature Index says shadow walkers do.

**First appearance:** World boss only for now (Netanel, 1 Nov 2026); he is freed in the world, not in a chapter. Found at the Grey Spire at the top of Elk Run (area 2.2). After each win the shadow leaves him, and it finds him again when he respawns.

## Appearance (placeholder; no chapter describes him yet)
- An enormous old elk bull, far bigger than a horse, with a shaggy silver-grey coat, a heavy mane and moss on his shoulders.
- Blackened, cracked antlers with black smoke drifting off the tips.
- Black smoke where his eyes should be.
- A hollow chest: ribs showing through torn hide, black smoke turning inside.
- Once freed: pale grey antlers, clear dark eyes, a long healed scar across the chest.

## Personality and voice
He doesn't speak. Even taken, he seems proud and sorrowful rather than cruel. He guards the Grey Spire and does not chase Eli past the Elk Path. Freed, he is calm and tired, and looks at Eli for a long moment before walking into the trees.

## Fight (for the game)
- **Antler charge:** a long rush in a straight line after he lowers his head and paws the ground. Step sideways.
- **Hoof stomp:** he rears and slams down, and a ring of smoke spreads along the ground. Dash through it or keep away.
- **Hollow bellow:** at two-thirds health he calls two Hollow Stags from the trees.
- **Shadow antlers:** at half health his antlers grow wider, and his charges and swings reach further.
- Defeat drives the shadow out instead of killing him.
- Plan numbers: base kind `hollow-stag`, health ×6, damage ×2, arena 520, respawn 45 minutes (before he is freed in the story).

## Relationships
- **The Elk Spirit:** He led its herd. The Spirit keeps the Listening Tree and can carry Eli toward the Ember Volcano. Freeing Greyhorn could be what earns that.
- **The Hollow Stags:** Infected deer and elk that follow his call.
- **Croshelda's shadow:** It took him, the same way it takes everything it infects.

## Hooks
- Freeing him could be a quest the Elk Spirit gives, or the reason the Spirit agrees to help.
- After he is freed, the Elk Run shadow walker share could drop, and he could appear as a peaceful elk at the Grey Spire.
