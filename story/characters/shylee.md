# Shylee - Character Sheet

**Role:** Eli's little sister, back home in our world.

**First appearance:** Chapter 1 (mentioned again in Chapter 5)

## Appearance (as stated)
- Age 18 (Ch1). She was 3 when their parents died.
- Wears earbuds; first seen on the couch (Ch1).
- When she doesn't want anyone to see her cry, she presses her lips together, a habit from childhood (Ch1).
- "for a second she looked a lot older than eighteen" (Ch1).
- No hair, eye or build details are given.

## Personality and voice
Dry and teasing, but deeply caring. Described as "tougher than me" by Eli. After the funeral she went back to school, friends and her life. She has been looking after Eli. Taking out one earbud is, for her, "the equivalent of a standing ovation."
- "Stepping out *where*? You haven't stepped out since the funeral. The delivery guy thinks you're dead." (Ch1)
- "Eli. I've been feeding *you* for two months." (Ch1)
- "Hey. Love you, dummy." (Ch1)

## Relationships
- **Eli:** Older brother. Close, with a teasing affection. She urged him to go.
- **Grandpa Oscar:** Raised her from age 3. He never took her to the island ("Still rude").

## What she knows / hooks
- Knows Eli went to Grandpa's fishing hut "for a couple of days." She expects a text and asked him to bring back "a big one" fish.
- Eli never texted. In Ch5 Eli imagines her waiting alone in the apartment, the way Grandpa used to wait on the dock.
- Eli: "I didn't think anything of it. You never do." This foreshadows a long separation.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Hair color, eye color, height, style.
- What she is doing now, and whether she will try to find Eli.
- Whether she knows anything about Grandpa's other world.

## Visual reference notes
18-year-old modern girl on a couch with earbuds and a phone. Her expression mixes teasing and quiet sadness. All physical features are undefined.
