# Eli - Character Sheet

**Role:** Narrator and protagonist. Grandson of Oscar; older brother of Shylee. Chosen by the Reedwater Region Core.

**First appearance:** Chapter 1

## Appearance (as stated)
- Age 27 (Ch1).
- Carries a backpack over one shoulder when he leaves home (Ch1).
- Has a phone; the captain wrote his number on the back of Eli's hand in marker (Ch1).
- Wears jeans; the lure sits in the pocket (Ch5, also Ch1-4).
- After the attack: aching all over, sleeping on a blanket in wet grass (Ch2).
- No hair, eye, skin, height or build details are given.

## Personality and voice
Self-mocking and funny, a shut-in who has "gotten very good at shutting myself inside" with video games and anime. Says he has no job, money or girlfriend. Avoids trouble by putting it off ("a problem for future me"). Wary of the uncanny (horror-movie rules) but acts on instinct when the voice speaks. A good cook, "one of the few things I was genuinely good at" (Ch1). Gets seasick. Feels deep, unspoken grief and regret about Grandpa: he never asked him much. Cries when Emilia says how proud Oscar was (Ch4).
- "...Screw it." (Ch1)
- "...That's a problem for future me." (Ch1)
- "Canoe. Raft. A really big log. I need to get off this island as soon as possible." (Ch2)

## Relationships
- **Grandpa Oscar:** Raised Eli from age 12, after Eli's parents died in a car crash. Took him fishing and told him stories. Took him to the island once, at age six or seven.
- **Shylee:** Little sister, 9 years younger. She has been feeding him for two months. They told each other "Love you" when he left. His main reason to get home.
- **Mara:** His rescuer. Teasing, a little awkward; he finds her pretty (Ch1-2).
- **Emilia:** Village chief. She says "Look how you've grown," as if she has met him before. She will train him for two full moons.
- **Boat captain:** Recognized Eli from his childhood trip.

## What he knows / secrets / hooks
- He took the Region Core (the glowing "lure") from the cabin when a young woman's voice said "Take it... You have to live" (Ch1). It pulses with his heartbeat and shocks him at key moments (Ch1, Ch4).
- Learns he is in another world, and that Grandpa's stories were memories (Ch4).
- Has a vision of a crying white-haired girl and screams "Croshelda" without knowing why. The voice tells him "Accept me" and "You have to save her" (Ch5).
- Swallows the core (Ch5). He then sees floating writing he can't read, a talking ocelot appears, and he passes out.
- Goal: Ember Volcano stones, reached via the Listening Tree. First, two months of training with Drayfus.
- He never sent Shylee the text he promised.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Hair color, eye color, height, build, skin tone, clothing beyond jeans and a backpack.
- What powers the Region Core gives him.
- Why Emilia seems to have met him before. Whether he remembers anything from his childhood visit.
- His parents' names. Emilia says Eli meant more to Oscar than "his own son," so Eli's father was Oscar's son.

## Visual reference notes
27-year-old modern guy in jeans with a backpack and phone. His physical look is entirely undefined, so the artist must not treat any choice as canon. His signature prop is the blue, glowing, amber-like worm "lure" (Region Core).
