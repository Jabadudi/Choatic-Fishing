# Croshelda - Character Sheet

**Role:** "The Witch of Darkness." The shadow walkers were her dark army. Seen by Eli in a vision as a crying girl.

**First appearance:** Named in Chapter 3. Seen in a vision in Chapter 5.

## Appearance (as stated, Ch5 vision)
- A girl with long white hair, "pearly and shining, falling all the way down her back."
- Purple eyes: "A deep, unreal purple, like the sky right before a storm."
- Crying.
- "Black light" exploded out of her: "A darkness that *moved*, an aura that burst outward from her body like a shockwave and swallowed everything around us."
- Note: the text never states that this girl is Croshelda. Eli screams the name in the vision without knowing her, then connects it to the Witch of Darkness.

## Personality and voice
- No dialogue. Shown only crying. Eli feels his chest "pulled so hard it hurt" on seeing her.

## Lore (Ch3, per Mara)
- "The shadow walkers... are what's left of the dark army that served Croshelda, the Witch of Darkness."
- Shadow walkers began coming back when Goldanir disappeared, 400 years ago.

## Relationships
- **Shadow walkers:** Her former army.
- **Eli:** Tied to him through the vision. The Region Core's voice says "You have to save her."
- **Goldanir:** Any link is implied only by the shared lore. Not stated.

## Hooks
- The tension: a feared "Witch of Darkness" in legend, yet a weeping girl whom the voice says Eli must save.
- In the vision, something huge grabbed Eli and hauled him into the sky away from her.
- Whether the vision is a memory, a premonition, or "something worse" (Eli's words).

## Open questions / not yet established
- Whether the girl really is Croshelda; whether she is alive now; her age; how a girl became the "Witch of Darkness."
- Who the "her" in "save her" is. Whose voice speaks from the core.
- What grabbed Eli in the vision.

## Visual reference notes
Girl with very long, pearly-white hair down her back and storm-purple eyes, crying, with an aura of moving black "anti-light" bursting outward. In-world legend frames her as the Witch of Darkness.
