# Emilia - Character Sheet

**Role:** Village chief, Mara's grandmother. Knew Oscar. Will be Eli's mentor and will arrange his training.

**First appearance:** Chapter 4 (referenced as "my grandma" in Chapter 2)

## Appearance (as stated)
- An old woman. Her face is "lined and brown from years of sun." Long silver hair, tied back (Ch4).
- Sits slowly, "the way old people sit when their knees have opinions" (Ch4).
- When she smiles, "a much younger woman looked out of that face. Long dark hair. Laughing at something just off to one side." Eli has "seen that laugh before" (Ch4).
- Her home is the biggest house in the village: timber and stone, painted deep red with white trim, a carved door frame, a covered porch. Two spear guards stand at the door, one with a scar through his eyebrow (Ch4).

## Personality and voice
Warm and maternal, with quiet authority. Her expression carries "the careful kind" of hope, "the kind that's been disappointed before." She giggles gently, without mockery, when Eli cries. When she gives orders, "the chief came back into her voice." She speaks of the elk spirit as ordinary fact.
- "Oh, my. Look how you've grown. I can't believe it's really you." (Ch4)
- "If you are here, then Oscar is gone." (Ch4)
- "Emilia. Just Emilia." (Ch4)

## Relationships
- **Mara:** Granddaughter.
- **Oscar:** Knew him well and deeply. She knew he came from another world and how he travelled. She says he "never stopped talking about" Eli.
- **Eli:** Acts as though she has met him before. She took his face in both hands. Plans to train him.
- **Drayfus:** Will begin Eli's training at her order.
- **The swordsman:** Was talking with her privately when Eli arrived.

## What she knows / secrets / hooks
- Explains the Region Core: the one Eli holds belongs to Reedwater, and only those the land chooses can resonate with one. It must be ingested (Ch4).
- Explains the route home: Ember Volcano stones (far northeast, beyond the Crystal Lakes), reached via the Listening Tree and its elk spirit at the edge of the Whispering Forest (Ch4).
- Mara calls the cabin "my grandma's old home" (Ch2).
- **Hint, not confirmed:** her younger self (long dark hair, laughing to one side) resembles the woman in Oscar's photo.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Whether she is the woman in the photo. Her relationship with Oscar, and whether she is connected to Eli's grandmother.
- Why she says "look how you've grown" to Eli.
- Her age, eye color, and Mara's parents.
- Why she sent the swordsman out, and what they discussed.
- Who kept the cabin clean.

## Visual reference notes
Elderly village chief with long silver hair tied back and a sun-lined, brown face. Warm presence at a hearth with a kettle and clay teapot. A younger-self variant should echo the photo woman (long dark hair, laughing off to the side). Her house: red-painted timber, white trim, carved door frame.
