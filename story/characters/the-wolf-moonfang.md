# Brume (the Moonfang wolf) - Character Sheet

**Role:** The huge gray wolf that saves Eli from the shadow walker during the storm. Oscar's old companion, who waited for him on the Reedwater shore. A Moonfang who is watching Eli and will choose to stay with him after the raid.

**First appearance:** Chapter 1 (the rescue; recalled in Chapters 2 and 4). Named and meets Eli face to face in Chapter 10.

## Appearance (as stated)
- "A wolf. Gray. Huge. Its head would have come up to my hip." (Ch1)
- Comes "out of the dark like a thrown boulder" (Ch2). Also "a wolf the size of a pony" (Ch2, Ch4).
- Silver fur "the color of old silver, the kind that's been handled for so many years that it has gone soft at the edges." Broad shoulders and a deep, heavy chest (Ch10).
- Eyes a cold, clear gray, "like water under ice" (Ch10).
- Moves without a sound. Smells of pine, rain and old smoke (Ch10).
- In the vision of Oscar's past, its fur is darker (Ch10).

## Personality and voice
- No speech. Calm, patient, deliberate.
- Lowers its head to bow, and looks at the empty air where Eli's ocelot sits, so it can see the ocelot (Ch10).
- Dryly playful once: when Oscar first arrived on the beach, it drove a fish ashore for him as a joke (per Emilia, Ch10).

## Backstory (per Emilia, Ch10)
- Has guarded the Reedwater shore for longer than Emilia has been alive.
- The first to find Oscar when he arrived in this world. Went out with Oscar on night rounds and taught him the shoreline.
- When Oscar stopped coming, he waited. He went to the cabin shore every night.
- On the night of the storm Mara chased a shadow walker toward the cabin but was too slow. Brume came out of the dark and hit it just before it reached Eli (Ch1, Ch2, Ch10). The story does not say how he knew; he is simply a keen, sharp, powerful wolf who saw the same thing Mara did.
- "The Moonfang choose once, and they choose slowly." He has not chosen Eli yet. He watches from the edge of the fields. He chooses to stay with Eli after the raid on Willowback, and carries Eli and Mara out (decided).

## Relationships
- **Eli:** Saved his life in Ch1. Touches his nose to the core in Ch10 and shares a vision of Oscar. Afterward he watches Eli from the edge of the fields during his laps, but does not stay close yet.
- **Oscar:** Closest companion. In the vision Oscar tells him "I'm sorry I took so long, old friend" and asks him to be there for Eli.
- **The ocelot:** Recognises it. The ocelot says it knew him "when he was a pup" (Ch10).
- **Drayfus:** Drayfus says Moonfang have not come inside the village fence in thirty years. Brume does.
- **Emilia:** She named him. "We gave him ours, and he tolerated it."

## Hooks
- Why he can see the ocelot, and how the ocelot knew him as a pup.
- Whether he is the wolf in Grandpa's photo (strongly implied).
- The promise Oscar asked of him, and what else Oscar said.

## Open questions / not yet established

- Markings or scars (a small notch in the left ear is used in art as a placeholder).
- Whether other Moonfang will also come into the village now.
- The escape (decided): Brume carries Eli and Mara out of the burning village, and the blacksmith's Rimejaw carries Drayfus.

## Visual reference notes
