# The Wolf (Moonfang) - Character Sheet

**Role:** The huge gray wolf that saves Eli from the shadow walker during the storm.

**First appearance:** Chapter 1 (recalled in Chapters 2 and 4)

## Appearance (as stated)
- "A wolf. Gray. Huge. Its head would have come up to my hip." (Ch1)
- Comes "out of the dark like a thrown boulder" (Ch2). Eli also calls it "a wolf the size of a pony" (Ch2, Ch4).

## Behavior
- Before the attack, Eli hears a wolf howling outside the cabin (Ch1).
- It shoots out of the darkness, slams into the axe-wielding shadow walker hard enough to knock him off his feet, and they vanish "into the mud together." Then come snarling and a shout, and the axe hits the ground (Ch1). Eli blacks out, and the wolf is not seen afterward.
- No voice or speech.

## Context: the Moonfang (per Mara, Ch2)
- "The wolves. They roam the land and keep its peace from the monsters of the night... They've guarded the villages for as long as anyone remembers."
- The Moonfang fought alongside the villagers against the shadow walker pack the same night.
- The text implies, but never states, that this wolf is one of the Moonfang.
- This echoes Grandpa's story about "wolves that walked the shoreline at night and kept the fishermen safe" (Ch1, Ch4).

## Relationships
- **Eli:** Saved his life.
- No other bond is stated.

## Open questions / not yet established
- **Is it the same wolf as in Grandpa's photo?** The photo shows a gray animal "so big I first mistook it for a rock," with its head at the young woman's hip. The photo is decades old. The resemblance is a hint, not a fact.
- Whether it is one individual with a name, or just one of the Moonfang.
- Where it went, and whether it was hurt.
- Whether it was guarding the cabin, or followed the shadow walker there.
- Eye color, markings, intelligence.

## Visual reference notes
Massive gray wolf, pony-sized, head at an adult's hip. Shown lunging out of a dark storm in a lightning flash. Eyes and markings are undefined. A photo variant: sitting rock-like at the feet of a young couple on a dock.
