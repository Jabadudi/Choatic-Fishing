# Grandpa Oscar - Character Sheet

**Role:** Eli and Shylee's late grandfather. He travelled between worlds. His bequest of the map starts the story.

**First appearance:** Chapter 1 (as "Grandpa"; first named "Oscar" in Chapter 4)

## Appearance (as stated)
- Older years: wore a fishing jacket and "smelling like the lake" (Ch1). Shaky handwriting (Ch1). Bad knees that eventually stopped his summer trips (Ch1).
- In the old photograph: a young man of "maybe twenty" on the cabin dock, "grinning like an idiot," holding up a fish nearly as long as his arm (Ch1).
- Eye and hair color, height and build are not given.

## Personality and voice
Warm, a storyteller who also roasts people. He told the same stories a hundred times without one detail changing. Deflects personal questions with humor. Went quiet when asked about Eli's grandmother. Eli says he would have complained about too much seasoning "purely so he wouldn't have to admit it was better."
- "You deserve more than four walls, kiddo. Go live a little. It changed my life. Maybe it'll change yours." (note on map, Ch1)
- "Nobody, kiddo. Just an old man getting older." (Ch1)
- "the kid's broke." (quoted by the captain, Ch1)

## Relationships
- **Eli and Shylee:** Raised them after their parents died. Emilia says Eli was "his treasure. More than his own son" (Ch4).
- **His son:** Eli and Shylee's father, implied (Ch4).
- **Boat captain:** Old friend. The captain calls him the "best fisherman I ever knew."
- **Emilia:** Knew him well. Says Oscar "never stopped talking about" Eli (Ch4).
- **Eli's grandmother:** Never discussed. Identity unknown.

## Secrets / hooks
- Came "from another world," according to Emilia. He used special stones from the slopes of the Ember Volcano to "gather enough mana to jump through space" (Ch4). Emilia calls him "a powerful and extraordinary man."
- His "bedtime stories" were memories of the other world: a stone road under a lake, wolves guarding fishermen at night, a girl who whistles fish into baskets (Ch1, Ch4).
- Took Eli to the island once, aged six or seven. Sat on the dock watching the trees "like he was waiting for somebody who was late." After that he went alone every summer (Ch1).
- The photo shows a woman with long dark hair beside him, and a huge gray wolf whose head reaches her hip. On the back are markings that match those inside the Region Core (Ch1).
- He left the Region Core in a lure box at the cabin. The cabin was clean and the rods freshly strung, though he hadn't been there in years.
- He told the captain Eli might come someday.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Full name/surname; age at death; cause of death.
- Who the woman in the photo is (possibly young Emilia; see Emilia sheet). Who Eli's grandmother is.
- How he first crossed over, and why he stopped bringing Eli.
- What the markings on the photo say.
- Whether he chose to leave the Region Core for Eli.
