# Corvane - Character Sheet

**Role:** A general of Croshelda's army ("a general of the Witch of Darkness"). He leads the shadow walker army that destroys Willowback.

**First appearance:** Chapter 14 (the voice in Eli's Chapter 11 dream is not confirmed to be his).

## Appearance (as stated, Ch14)
- Tall, taller than Drayfus.
- Armor so dark it doesn't reflect firelight; "it just swallowed it."
- Unlike the shadow walkers, he has a real face: pale and narrow, almost handsome, with hollow cheeks.
- Eyes "like two chips of black glass."
- Speaks with one voice, calm and almost polite (the dream voice was many voices layered).
- His armor cracks and frosts under Emilia's ice.

## Personality and voice
Calm, courteous, patient, utterly certain. He treats the battle as a formality. Eli finds his face "so much worse than no face at all."
- "I am Corvane, a general of the Witch of Darkness. My lady sends her regards." (Ch14)
- "She has a long memory. And this village has something of hers. Again." (Ch14)
- "*There* you are. She was right. You do shine." (Ch14)
- "Old woman. You remember. Good. Then you know how this ends." (Ch14)
- "Find the boy." (Ch14)

## Relationships
- **Croshelda:** Serves her and calls her "my lady." He came to collect what Willowback "took" from her (see the debt note in the arc plan).
- **Emilia:** Recognizes her as someone who remembers the old debt. He walks on her as she falls; a shadow walker strikes the killing blow, not him.
- **Eli:** His target. He knows Eli by his "shine," the core's light.
- **The shadow walkers:** They part for him and stop when he walks out.

## Hooks
- "Again" implies Croshelda's army has come for something in Willowback before.
- "She was right. You do shine" suggests Croshelda herself has seen or sensed Eli.
- After the raid he is hunting Eli and the core.

## Open questions / not yet established
- Whether he was the layered voice in the Chapter 11 dream, or only speaks for it.
- Whether he was once human, and how many generals Croshelda has.
- His weapon. None is shown in Ch14.
- Whether he survives Volume 1.
