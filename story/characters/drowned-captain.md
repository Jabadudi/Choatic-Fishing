# The Drowned Captain - Character Sheet

**Role:** World boss of the Drowned Road. A shadow walker officer of Croshelda's army who went down with the old road when it flooded, and still holds it.

**First appearance:** None yet. A world boss from the spawn plan, found in the colonnade on the Drowned Road (area 16).

## Appearance (placeholder; no chapter describes him yet)
- Tall, taller than Drayfus, standing straight like a soldier on parade.
- Faceless like every shadow walker: a smooth black shadow under a rusted open-faced officer's helm with a broken plume.
- Rusted plate armor crusted with barnacles and shells, a tarnished brass gorget.
- A soaked, ragged navy captain's cloak hung with river weed, always dripping.
- Black smoke seeps from the armor joints. A long, curved, corroded sabre with a brass basket hilt.

## Personality and voice
Silent. He fights like a drilled officer: a salute when he rises, measured steps, no wasted swings. He never chases Eli out of the colonnade. He is still guarding the road he drowned on.

## Fight (for the game)
- Rises from the shallows when Eli enters the colonnade.
- **Sabre sweep:** a wide arc in front of him after a clear wind-up. Step back or dash through.
- **Tide lunge:** a long thrust that slides him through the shallows. Get out of the line.
- **Call the drowned:** at two-thirds and one-third health he raises the sabre and two Drowned Soldiers rise from the water.
- Plan numbers: base kind `drowned-soldier`, health ×5, damage ×1.8, arena 600, respawn 30 minutes.

## Relationships
- **Croshelda:** Served in her army four hundred years ago, around the time Goldanir disappeared.
- **The Drowned Soldiers:** His men. They rise when he calls and wade the shallows that stop Eli.

## Hooks
- Why the old road flooded, and whether the army was marching on Willowback when it did.
- His rusted helm could be a trophy or a quest item for the village.

## Visual reference notes
Rust orange, barnacle white, brass and weed green on a black shadow body, always wet. The silhouette is a tall soldier with a plumed helm and a long curved sabre held low.
