# World rules (decided)

Settled by Netanel while drafting chapters 6–8. Add to this file as more are decided.

## Mana
- **Everyone has mana.** Not everyone can shape it.
- Max mana varies from person to person. Some are born with very little, some with tremendous amounts.
- Manipulating mana takes talent and awareness. Some are born talented, some get there through long training.
- Anyone observant enough to tune in to their own body can sense their mana. Drayfus, who is not a caster, can feel it after a long fight.

## The hollow
- The hollow is the felt sensation of mana running low: an emptiness behind the breastbone, as if something had been scooped out.
- It is a world mechanic, not a power. It is how a person feels their own reserves, and Drayfus teaches Eli to heed it.
- Ignoring it ends with collapse (chapter 6, night scene).

## Strength and power
- Strength lets a person afford power. A weak body pays for a cast out of mana alone, so reserves empty fast. A strong body carries part of the load (chapter 6, the box lesson).

## Region Core abilities (Eli, so far)
- **Still Water:** awakened when Eli takes the core (chapter 6).
- **Reedwater's Call:** awakens when Eli picks up Grandpa's rod (chapter 6). The line bends toward the caster's purpose, and casting costs less in living water (chapter 8).
- Two further abilities are locked.

## Learning magic
- Mana theory is explained gradually in the story, as Eli is taught.
- **Eli's first real magic lessons come from the elk spirit** at the Listening Tree, north of Willowback. Until then he only has the instinctive abilities the core gives him.
- So during the training arc (chapters 7–12), Eli and the village talk about mana in plain terms like "reserves" and "the hollow" but do not give a full theory.
